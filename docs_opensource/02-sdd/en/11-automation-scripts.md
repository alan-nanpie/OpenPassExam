# 11. Automation Scripts

## 1. Google Play Books & Audiobook Toolchain
- **`fetch_firestore_books.py`**: Exports approved questions from Google Cloud Firestore.
- **`build_ccna_epub.py`**: Compiles EPUB 3 eBooks with audio accessibility markup.
- **`publish_to_play_books.py`**: Packages ONIX 3.0 metadata for Google Play Books publication.

## 2. RAG Knowledge Pack Toolchain
- **`extract_gcs_rag_knowledge.py`**: Distills 6,688 textbook chunks and uploads them to Google Cloud Storage.
